About

I am an Assistant Professor in the Division of Electrical and Electronics Engineering at Karunya Institute of Technology and Sciences, Coimbatore, India. I received my Ph.D. in Information and Communication Engineering from Shenzhen University, China, where my thesis addressed resource allocation in wireless-powered communication networks.

My research focuses on resource allocation and optimization for wireless-powered communication networks (WPCNs) assisted by intelligent reflecting surfaces (IRS), with applications to the Internet of Things and integrated sensing and communication (ISAC). My background spans electrical power engineering, renewable energy and wireless communications, with academic and professional experience in India and China.
