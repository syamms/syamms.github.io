About

I am an Assistant Professor in the Division of Electrical and Electronics Engineering at Karunya Institute of Technology and Sciences, Coimbatore. My research focuses on resource allocation for intelligent reflecting surface (IRS)-assisted wireless-powered communication networks (WPCNs), with applications to the Internet of Things (IoT) and integrated sensing and communication (ISAC).

I received my Ph.D. for work on wireless-powered communication networks, and my M.Tech research was on grid-connected photovoltaic systems. I also serve as the Exam Coordinator for the Division of EEE.
