About

Dr. Syam M. S. is an Assistant Professor in the Division of Electrical and Electronics Engineering at Karunya Institute of Technology and Sciences, Coimbatore, India. He earned his Ph.D. in Information and Communication Engineering from Shenzhen University, China. His Ph.D. thesis was titled "Research on Resource Allocation in Wireless-Powered Communication Networks". In particular, his research focused on resource allocation and optimization for wireless-powered communication networks (WPCNs), including WPCNs assisted by intelligent reflecting surfaces (IRS). This research has applications to the Internet of Things and integrated sensing and communication (ISAC). His background spans electrical power engineering, renewable energy and wireless communications, with academic and professional experience in India and China.

Prospective research students and collaborators are welcome to get in touch.

Research Interests

  • Wireless-powered communication networks (WPCNs)
  • Intelligent reflecting surfaces (IRS / RIS)
  • Integrated sensing and communication (ISAC)
  • Internet of Things
  • Energy-efficient resource allocation; convex and non-convex optimization
  • Grid-connected photovoltaic systems
