Useful Links

A collection of links I find useful for checking where a journal is indexed, how it is ranked, and for finding papers.

Journal Indexes

  • To check if a journal is SCI, SCIE or SSCI indexed:
    1. Open the Web of Science Master Journal List.
    2. Search for the journal title.
    3. If the result shows "Web of Science Core Collection: Science Citation Index", "Science Citation Index Expanded" or "Social Sciences Citation Index", the journal is indexed in SCI, SCIE or SSCI respectively.
  • Scopus: search the journal title in Scopus Sources to see whether it is indexed and view its CiteScore.
  • EI Compendex: Compendex website
    To download the list of EI journals and proceedings in Excel format, open the Compendex website and click on "Compendex source list".

Journal Rankings

  • JCR (Journal Citation Reports): ranking and impact factor of a journal (requires a subscription). Open JCR
  • SCImago Journal Rank (SJR): quartiles Q1–Q4. Open SCImago
    This ranking is different from the JCR ranking and the Chinese Academy of Sciences ranking.
  • Chinese Academy of Sciences (CAS) ranking: quartiles Q1–Q4, available through LetPub.
    This ranking is different from the SCImago and JCR rankings.
  • China Computer Federation (CCF) ranking: available on MyHuiBan.
  • Citations in Web of Science: Open Web of Science
