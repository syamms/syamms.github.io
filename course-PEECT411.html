Course Objective

To introduce the basic concepts of electronic measuring instruments.

Course Structure

ModuleContentsHours
1Introduction to Measuring Instruments
Generalized configurations and functional elements of instrumentation systems; need for measurement systems; classification of measuring instruments; static and dynamic characteristics of measuring instruments.		9
2Sensors and Transducers
Classification and selection criteria of transducers. Principles, construction, theory, advantages, disadvantages and applications of resistive transducers (potentiometers, metallic and semiconductor strain gauges, resistance thermometers, thermistors), inductive transducers (LVDT) and capacitive transducers (capacitive microphone).		9
3Electronic Measuring Instruments
Digital storage oscilloscope; working principle and applications of waveform analyser, digital frequency meter, harmonic distortion meter, harmonic analyser, spectrum analyser and logic state analyser; IEEE-488 General Purpose Interface Bus (GPIB) instruments with applications; EMI, grounding and shielding.		9
4PLC Programming
Programming ON/OFF inputs; ladder diagrams; register basics; PLC timers and counters; arithmetic and number comparison functions; data handling functions; skip and master control relay functions; jump with non-return and return; data table, register and move functions; PLC functions with bits.		9
